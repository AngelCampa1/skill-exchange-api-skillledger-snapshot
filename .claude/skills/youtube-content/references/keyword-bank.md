## Title writing reference

### Structural patterns that work

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| How + without cash | "How a Freelancer Got Full Branding Without Paying Cash" |
| Number + tradeable skills | "7 Services Freelancers Can Trade Instead of Paying For" |
| Bootstrap angle | "How Bootstrapped Founders Get Skills Without a Budget" |
| Comparison | "SkillLedger vs. Fiverr: Trade or Pay?" |
| Case study | "How a Developer Got $2,000 of Design Work for Free" |
| Educational concept | "What Is Skill Exchange? The Freelance Alternative to Cash" |

### Title avoid list
- Generic: "Freelance Collaboration Tips" (no hook)
- Question-only: "Can You Trade Skills?" (too vague)
- Corporate: "SkillLedger Platform Overview" (screams ad)
- Feature-led: "SkillLedger's Credit System Explained" (nobody searches this first)

## Competitor channel landscape (skill exchange YouTube)

Top YouTube results for skill exchange and freelance collaboration topics are dominated by:
- Generic freelancing advice channels (Fiverr tips, Upwork strategies)
- Generic startup/entrepreneurship channels without skill exchange focus
- No dedicated skill barter/credit economy platform channel exists

The SEO opportunity is high because keywords like "skill exchange platform" and "trade skills for services" are low competition with growing search intent. The "bootstrapped startup" and "build without money" keyword clusters are high-competition but can be entered via specific angle differentiation.

## Algorithm notes

- Primary keyword in the first 3 words of the title
- Spoken transcript indexed — naturally weave "skill exchange," "credit economy," "trade skills," "freelance collaboration" into scripts
- Timestamps/chapters rank independently — name them with secondary keywords
- Mid-roll CTAs outperform end-only CTAs
- Slingshot Strategy: thumbnail + title changes alone can lift CTR by 50-87%
