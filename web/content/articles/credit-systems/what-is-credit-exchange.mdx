## What Is a Credit Exchange System?

A credit exchange system lets professionals earn and spend virtual currency, called credits, in place of cash. Rather than invoicing a client and waiting 30 days, you complete a project and receive credits instantly. Those credits can then be spent on any service offered on the platform.

SkillLedger's credit system is designed to make professional service trading straightforward and low-friction.

## How SkillLedger Credits Work

Credits on SkillLedger represent units of professional value. Each credit corresponds to a standardized unit of service time, stored in your [credit wallet](/glossary/credit-wallet), making it simple to compare and exchange work across different skill types.

**Earning credits:**
- Set your hourly credit rate when you create your profile
- Complete projects for other platform members
- Credits are released from escrow once the client approves your work

**Spending credits:**
- Browse the marketplace for services you need
- Submit a project request with agreed credit terms
- Credits are held in escrow until you approve the delivered work

## Credit Exchange vs. Direct Barter

Traditional barter requires a "double coincidence of wants," meaning both parties must want what the other offers at the same time. Credit exchange removes this constraint:

| Feature | Direct Barter | Credit Exchange |
|---------|--------------|-----------------|
| Timing flexibility | Must trade simultaneously | Trade with anyone, anytime |
| Partner matching | Must find exact match | Access entire marketplace |
| Rate differences | Difficult to handle | Credits bridge the gap |
| Trust mechanism | Personal | Platform-verified escrow |

## Setting Your Credit Rate

Your credit rate should reflect your market value. Consider:

1. **Research comparable services** on the platform to understand market rates
2. **Factor in your experience level.** Verified professionals with strong reputations can charge premium rates
3. **Account for project complexity.** Routine tasks vs. specialized work merit different rates
4. **Review and adjust** as your reputation score grows

## The Escrow System

SkillLedger holds credits in [escrow](/glossary/escrow) during active projects. This protects both parties:

- **As a service provider**: You know credits exist before you start work
- **As a client**: Your credits are only released when you approve the delivered work

If a dispute arises, SkillLedger's resolution process reviews the project scope and delivered work to determine a fair outcome.

## Tax Considerations

Credit exchanges may have tax implications. In the United States, the IRS considers barter income taxable at fair market value. See how SkillLedger [compares to other exchange platforms](/compare/skillledger-vs-simbi) for differences in how credits and taxes are handled. SkillLedger provides transaction records to help with reporting. Always consult a qualified tax professional for advice specific to your situation and jurisdiction.
