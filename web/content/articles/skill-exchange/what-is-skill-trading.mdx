## Skill Trading: The Modern Professional Exchange

Skill trading (also called service swapping, skill bartering, or professional exchange) is when two or more professionals trade their expertise instead of paying with cash.

In its simplest form: a UX designer needs SEO help, an SEO specialist needs a new website. They trade skills. Both get what they need without spending money.

## Why Skill Trading Is Growing

Freelancing has created a paradox: professionals with highly valuable skills often struggle to afford other skilled professionals. A junior developer earning $60/hour cannot easily afford $150/hour legal advice. Skill trading bridges that gap.

**Three trends driving growth:**

1. **The freelance economy.** 76 million Americans freelanced in 2024, creating a massive pool of tradeable expertise.
2. **Cash flow pressure.** Small businesses and solopreneurs constantly balance investing in growth versus preserving runway.
3. **Community over transactions.** Professionals increasingly value relationships over purely transactional work.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I have to find a direct swap partner?
No. Modern platforms use credit systems. You earn credits from anyone, spend them with anyone. No direct swap required.

### What if someone's skill is worth more than mine?
Credits compensate for rate differences. If you charge 50 credits/hour and need a service that costs 75 credits/hour, you either supplement with credits you have earned elsewhere or negotiate a longer engagement.
