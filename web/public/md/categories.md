## How Category Exchange Works

Each category has professionals who set their own credit rates based on market value. Browse any category to find professionals, view their portfolios and reviews, and propose exchanges using SkillLedger credits.

## Cross-Category Exchange

SkillLedger's credit system makes cross-category exchange seamless. A web developer can earn credits from a marketing project and spend them on legal services. Credits work across all 19 categories.
