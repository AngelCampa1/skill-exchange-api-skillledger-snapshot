# About SkillLedger

> A professional collaboration platform where freelancers and businesses exchange services using credits instead of cash.

## Mission

SkillLedger exists to make professional services accessible through fair, transparent skill exchange. No commissions, no hidden fees — credits map directly to fair market value.

## How SkillLedger Works

1. **Post Your Skills** - List your professional skills and set your credit rate based on market value
2. **Exchange Services** - Find professionals who need your skills and offer what you need in return. Escrow protects both sides.
3. **Build Reputation** - Complete exchanges, earn reviews, and grow your reputation score

## Core Values

- **Trust**: Every exchange protected by escrow, verified reviews, and reputation scores
- **Fairness**: Credits map to fair market value so all professionals receive equitable exchanges
- **Community**: 19 skill categories connecting professionals across disciplines
- **Transparency**: Credit rates, reputation scores, and exchange terms visible to both parties

## Tax Compliance

The IRS treats barter exchanges as taxable income at fair market value under Revenue Ruling 79-24. SkillLedger automatically tracks FMV for every exchange and generates 1099-B-ready documentation for year-end tax reporting.

## How SkillLedger Differs from Fiverr or Upwork

Fiverr and Upwork are cash marketplaces that charge 10-27% in fees. SkillLedger is a credit-based exchange platform — you trade skills directly with no commissions. You keep the full value of your work.

## Who Is SkillLedger For?

Freelancers, consultants, and small businesses who want to exchange professional services without cash. Whether you are a developer who needs design work or a marketer who needs legal review, skill exchange gives you access to expertise by offering your own.
