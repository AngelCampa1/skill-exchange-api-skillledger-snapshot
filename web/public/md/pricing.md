## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is there a free trial?**
Yes. Every plan includes a 30-day free trial. A credit card is required to start. Cancel within 30 days for a full refund — no questions asked.

**What payment methods do you accept?**
All major credit cards, debit cards, and ACH bank transfers through Stripe. PCI-compliant encryption on all payments.

**Can I cancel anytime?**
Yes. Cancel from account settings. Your plan features stay active through your current billing period. No cancellation fees or long-term contracts.

**Do I need a paid plan for escrow protection?**
Yes. Escrow protection is included on all paid plans. It applies to every exchange regardless of tier.

**Is the credit exchange rate different by plan?**
No. Credit rates are set by each professional based on market value. Your plan tier does not change credit valuation — it affects platform features and limits.
