# How SkillLedger Works

> A step-by-step guide to exchanging professional services on SkillLedger's credit-based barter platform.

## Step 1: Create Your Profile

Sign up and list your professional skills. Set your credit rate based on your market value. Your profile acts as your portfolio — showcase your expertise so other professionals know what you bring to the table.

## Step 2: Browse the Marketplace

Search across 19 skill categories to find professionals who offer the services you need. Filter by category, location, credit rate, and reputation score. View profiles, portfolios, and reviews before proposing an exchange.

## Step 3: Propose an Exchange

When you find a match, propose a project with clear scope, timeline, and credit terms. Both parties review and agree before work begins. Credits enter escrow automatically when the exchange is accepted.

## Step 4: Complete the Work

Deliver your professional services according to the agreed terms. Use built-in messaging and collaboration tools to stay aligned throughout the project.

## Step 5: Release Credits

When both parties confirm the work is complete and satisfactory, credits transfer automatically from escrow. If a dispute arises, SkillLedger's structured resolution process with neutral mediators ensures a fair outcome.

## Step 6: Build Your Reputation

After each exchange, both parties leave reviews. Your reputation score grows with every successful exchange, unlocking better opportunities and higher visibility in the marketplace.

## The Credit System

- Credits represent fair market value for professional services
- Each professional sets their own credit rate
- When you complete work, you earn credits equal to the agreed value
- Spend credits to hire other professionals
- No cash changes hands, no commissions taken

## Escrow Protection

Every exchange on SkillLedger is protected by built-in escrow:
- Credits are locked when both parties accept the exchange
- Credits release only when both sides confirm satisfaction
- Dispute resolution available if issues arise

## Tax Compliance

SkillLedger tracks fair market value for every exchange and generates 1099-B-ready documentation. The IRS treats barter exchanges as taxable income — SkillLedger handles the record-keeping automatically.
