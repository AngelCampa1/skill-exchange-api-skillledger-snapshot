# SkillLedger - Professional Collaboration Platform

> Exchange professional services across 19 skill categories in 50 US cities. 30-day free trial — no cash, no commissions.

## What is SkillLedger?

SkillLedger is a credit-based professional barter exchange where freelancers, consultants, and small businesses trade services without cash. You earn credits by providing your expertise and spend them to access services you need.

## How It Works

1. **Post Your Skills** - Create a profile listing your professional skills and set your credit rate based on market value.
2. **Exchange Services** - Browse the marketplace, propose exchanges with clear scope and timelines. Built-in escrow protects both parties.
3. **Build Reputation** - Complete exchanges, earn reviews, and grow your reputation score. Higher reputation unlocks better opportunities.

## Key Features

- **Credit Wallet**: Earn and spend credits mapped to fair market value
- **Escrow Protection**: Credits held until both parties confirm satisfaction
- **Reputation System**: Trust scores based on completed exchanges and reviews
- **19 Skill Categories**: Web development, design, marketing, writing, legal, accounting, and more
- **50 US Cities**: Local and remote service exchange across major metro areas
- **1099-B Compliance**: Automatic FMV tracking for IRS barter tax reporting

## Pricing

- **Professional ($19/mo)**: Core exchange features, escrow protection, 30-day free trial
- **Business ($49/mo)**: Unlimited exchanges, priority matching, advanced analytics, higher credit limits, 30-day free trial
- **Enterprise ($99/mo)**: Compliance reporting, team management, dedicated support, 30-day free trial

## Who Is It For?

- Freelancers who want to access professional services without cash outlay
- Consultants looking to expand their network through skill trading
- Small businesses that need services but want to conserve cash
- Professionals in any of 19 skill categories
