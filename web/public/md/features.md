# SkillLedger Platform Features

> Credit wallet exchange, project escrow, reputation badges, real-time collaboration, and a skill marketplace for professionals.

## Credit Wallet

Earn and spend credits mapped to fair market value. Each professional sets their own rate. Credits transfer automatically when exchanges complete. Track your balance, transaction history, and earnings in one dashboard.

## Project Escrow

Every exchange is protected by built-in escrow. Credits lock when both parties accept an exchange and release only when both sides confirm satisfaction. Structured dispute resolution with neutral mediators handles edge cases.

## Reputation & Badges

Trust scores based on completed exchanges, reviews, and platform history. Earn badges for milestones like first exchange, 10 completed projects, and category expertise. Higher reputation means better visibility and matching priority.

## Skill Marketplace

Browse 19 professional skill categories. Filter by category, location, credit rate, and reputation. View professional profiles with portfolios, reviews, and exchange history before proposing a project.

## Real-Time Collaboration

Built-in messaging and workspace tools for managing active exchanges. Share files, track milestones, and communicate directly within the platform. No need for external project management tools.

## Priority Matching (Premium)

Advanced algorithm matches you with the best-fit professionals based on skills, availability, location, and exchange history. Premium members get priority placement in search results and matching.

## Advanced Analytics (Premium)

Track your exchange performance, credit flow, category demand trends, and marketplace insights. Understand which skills are most in-demand and optimize your credit rate.

## 1099-B Tax Compliance

Automatic fair market value tracking for every exchange. SkillLedger generates 1099-B-ready documentation so you can report barter income accurately at tax time. Required by the IRS under Revenue Ruling 79-24.
